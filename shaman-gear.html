How to use this

This is the Shaman gear layer for the 1–60 companion. Your friend is primarily Resto, so the default advice is healing gear and 1H + shield. Enhancement and Elemental are included when a piece or weapon is better for leveling.

Forever data note: the current Forever client lists Daggers, Fist Weapons, Axes, Maces, Staves, Two-Handed Axes and Two-Handed Maces for Shaman. Mail becomes usable at 40 and shields are available from level 1. Dual Wield is still flagged as unconfirmed client-table data, so it is intentionally not used as a gearing recommendation.

🌊 Resto — default

Prioritize: +Healing, Intellect, mana/regen and useful survivability.

For regular dungeon healing, keep a dedicated 1H + shield set rather than relying on a melee weapon.

🪓 Enhancement

Prioritize: weapon damage first, then melee stats. Forever has a dedicated Two-Handed Mastery ability, so 2H weapons deserve a real checkpoint rather than being treated as a Classic-only curiosity.

⚡ Elemental

Prioritize: spell damage, Intellect and mana. Healing/caster crossover pieces are useful when the Shaman is still healing the duo.

🎯 Level Checkpoints

1–19
Use the best staff or 1H + shield available.

Do not spend big gold chasing marginal upgrades. For Resto, shield + useful stats matter more than a flashy DPS weapon.

20+
Check 2H axe / mace access.

Forever data has the Shaman Two-Handed Axes and Maces ability, separate from the old Classic Enhancement talent.

30–40
Start separating healing and melee sets.

Windfury arrives at 30 and mail becomes usable at 40. This is where an Enhancement weapon upgrade can matter much more.

40+
Mail + dungeon healing gear.

At 40, review every mail healing piece and keep a 1H + shield dungeon set.

50–60
Endgame weapon + healing set audit.

Stop treating every upgrade as universal. Keep separate roles where the itemization supports it.

🏰 Dungeon Integration

RFC

First dungeon checkpoint. Stack the quests; only stop for Shaman gear if a useful healing/caster or weapon upgrade appears.

Wailing Caverns

Check the Crescent Staff candidate, but do not force repeated runs just for it.

Razorfen Kraul

Watch the Corpsemaker candidate for Enhancement; also check healing/caster rewards before leaving.

Scarlet Monastery

Armory is the major 30s weapon checkpoint; Cathedral adds another weapon check around the high 30s.

Maraudon

Check both healing/caster and 2H options. Do not assume one item is best for every Shaman role.

BRD

Late-game weapon, healing and profession overlap. Keep the gear audit separate from the Rogue's weapon route.

🧠 Shaman Gear Rules

Resto

+Healing / Intellect / mana sustain first. A modest healing upgrade is more useful to the duo than a high-damage weapon sitting in the bag.

Enhancement

Weapon damage is the first question. Forever's Two-Handed Mastery makes 2H weapon checkpoints especially relevant.

Elemental

Spell damage + Intellect + mana. Healing crossover gear is fine while the character remains the group's primary healer.

Do not chase

Do not burn an hour farming a low-level weapon when the next dungeon or zone is about to offer another upgrade.
