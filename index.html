Professions

⛏ Mining

Keep it 1–60. Mine naturally while travelling and save useful ore for Engineering.

🦌 Skinning

Early gold/material profession. Skin while questing without detouring constantly.

⚙ Engineering

Planned PvP-focused swap. Drop Skinning once your gold/material stockpile and leveling stage make the switch worthwhile.

🩹 First Aid

Keep current. It reduces Rogue downtime while solo.

🍖 Cooking

Keep useful and always carry food.

🎣 Fishing

Optional downtime activity. Fish while waiting/travelling; never let it slow 1–60.

Recommended: Mining + Skinning early → Skinning → Engineering later → Mining + Engineering for PvP/endgame.
