📚 Truthseeker’s Bow — top priority

Library Books create a three-step gear path: 10 books for an Agility/Stamina neck, 20 for the +7 Agility/+7 Stamina Field Researcher’s Loop ring, then 25 for Truthseeker’s Bow. 🎯 MM vs BM

MM is the default by preference and expected group play. BM is the solo/duo fallback if pet damage and reduced downtime make the loop smoother. Reassess at level 40 if Dual Spec is available in the current client.

🏰 Dungeon rule

Run useful dungeons once after stacking quests. Wailing Caverns, Blackfathom Deeps and Razorfen Kraul are key weapon checks; don’t repeatedly farm rare drops unless your group is already going.

⚔️ Horde PvP

Contested-zone risk around Stonetalon, Ashenvale and Hillsbrad (roughly 15–20). Planned BG checkpoints: WSG 10, AB 20, Darkspear Islands 30 and AV 51; verify current honor/rank rules in client.

⛏️ Profession plan

Hunter 1–39: Mining + Skinning. Around 40, consider Engineering + Leatherworking only if your Tauren Druid can supply ore, stone and leather. If gold is short, keep gathering longer rather than buying a full 1–300 kit.

🏛️ Legacy and alts

Start with Well Rested 5/5. Legacy points are account-wide earned and separately spent per character (16 point cap in current system). Roster: Orc Hunter main; Tauren Druid Mining + Skinning; bank Mage Tailoring + Enchanting; all Horde PvP.

🎯 What matters for Combat / PvP

Weapons

Highest priority. Compare actual weapon damage, speed and useful stats; train the new weapon skill immediately.

Shoulders

Do not skip them just because weapon upgrades are more important. Track major leather agility/stamina pieces and set-piece upgrades.

Head / Chest / Legs

Large armor/stat-budget slots. A strong quest or dungeon piece can be worth taking even when it is not a famous Classic item.

Neck / Rings / Trinkets

Easy to overlook because they are not armor slots. Track meaningful Agility/AP/crit/hit/Expertise or proc upgrades as Forever itemization becomes verified.

Cloak / Wrist / Belt / Boots

Usually smaller individual upgrades, but still worth checking when the route hands one to you.

Ranged

Keep a current bow/gun/crossbow/thrown option. It is utility and pull/control gear, not a replacement for your melee weapon priority.

🧭 Dungeon Run Rule

1. PREP

Collect every quest that naturally fits the run and finish prerequisites before travelling to the entrance.

2. ENTER

Run at the recommended level unless the quest/reward is worth an earlier trip.

3. LOOT

Prioritize meaningful weapon/gear upgrades and quest drops. Do not farm low-value bosses just because they can drop something.

4. LEAVE COMPLETE

Turn-ins, Legacy credit, keys and follow-up quests are part of the dungeon run.
