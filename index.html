🎯 What matters for Combat / PvP

Weapons

Highest priority. Compare actual weapon damage, speed and useful stats; train the new weapon skill immediately.

Shoulders

Do not skip them just because weapon upgrades are more important. Track major leather agility/stamina pieces and set-piece upgrades.

Head / Chest / Legs

Large armor/stat-budget slots. A strong quest or dungeon piece can be worth taking even when it is not a famous Classic item.

Neck / Rings / Trinkets

Easy to overlook because they are not armor slots. Track meaningful Agility/AP/crit/hit/Expertise or proc upgrades as Forever itemization becomes verified.

Cloak / Wrist / Belt / Boots

Usually smaller individual upgrades, but still worth checking when the route hands one to you.

Ranged

Keep a current bow/gun/crossbow/thrown option. It is utility and pull/control gear, not a replacement for your melee weapon priority.

⚠️ Forever Gear Rules

Do not blindly chase Classic BiS

Forever has redone boss loot tables, added new quest rewards and added new world-drop/rare-creature items.

Keep the checkpoint philosophy

If the current item is good enough, do not spend an hour farming a marginal upgrade. Watch the next meaningful checkpoint instead.

BiS is a future layer

Once Forever's level-60 loot tables stabilize, this tab can promote verified endgame pieces into explicit BiS checkpoints instead of relying on Classic lists.

🧠 Gold Rules

🗡️ Rogue

Skin beasts, mine nodes and Pick Pocket humanoids when it costs no extra travel. Stealth lets you check dangerous rare-spawn areas without committing to a fight.

🌊 Shaman

Herbalism turns normal travel into income. Keep herbs that feed the Alchemy route instead of automatically selling every stack.

🤝 Duo

Do not split just because two theoretical farms exist. Follow the shared leveling route and collect materials from the same path whenever possible.

⚠️ Stop Rule

Do not stay after the zone stops giving useful XP or the material stops overlapping your route. Gold farming should be an add-on to leveling until a deliberate farm is called out.

⚔️ World PvP Command Center

This is the PvP route layer for the Undead Rogue + Troll Shaman duo. It should tell us when PvP naturally intersects the leveling route, not send us on random detours looking for fights.

10–20
Early contested play

Watch Hillsbrad and nearby Horde/Alliance travel routes. Rogue stealth gives scouting and escape options; Shaman sustain helps the duo reset.

20–40
Core world-PvP window

Hillsbrad, Arathi and Stranglethorn routes naturally create more player contact. Protect quest momentum and choose fights deliberately.

40–60
Route-intersection PvP

Contested-zone travel, dungeon entrances and quest hubs become the priority. Don't abandon a strong XP route just to hunt players.
