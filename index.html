Professions & gold

Crafting

First Aid / Cooking / Fishing

Track training, recipe checkpoints, consumables and low-friction opportunities while traveling or waiting.

Gold farms

Prefer reliable open-world material farming during leveling; treat rare BoEs, recipes and schematic drops as bonuses rather than guaranteed income.
