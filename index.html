🌿 Herbalism + ⚗️ Alchemy

Keep both professions active from the start. Pick herbs naturally along the quest route, turn useful materials into leveling consumables, and avoid detouring across the map just to chase low-value nodes.

🛡️ Healing Set

Keep the best practical healing pieces you find. Forever's itemization is changed enough that live item data should decide exact upgrades instead of old Classic lists.

⚔️ Questing Set

Maintain enough offensive gear to quest efficiently. Do not make every gear slot a healer-only slot while solo.

🧪 Alchemy Rule

Use Alchemy to support actual play: healing/mana consumables, useful buffs and duo supplies. Keep some herbs instead of converting everything immediately if a later recipe checkpoint is expected.

🌿 Gathering Rule

Take herbs that are on your route. If a node requires a major detour, keep moving unless the material is specifically needed for an important recipe.

Shaman role in the duo: you do not need to heal every second. Heal when required, maintain the Rogue's momentum, use totems intelligently, and add damage when the pull is under control.

🤝 Undead Rogue + Troll Shaman — Duo Route

Core idea: the Rogue supplies stealth, interrupts, crowd control and burst pressure; the Shaman supplies healing, totems, purge/support and emergency recovery. You can quest together without either character becoming dependent on the other, and you can split briefly when one character has a profession or quest objective to finish.

🗡️ Rogue Job

Pick targets, open from Stealth, interrupt dangerous casts, use Sap/other control when useful, and keep the Shaman safe. Do not overpull simply because healing is available.

🌊 Shaman Job

Keep the Rogue moving with efficient healing and totems. Contribute damage between healing needs. Use utility to turn difficult pulls into manageable ones.

👥 Add a Mage

A Mage turns the duo into a strong three-player core: extra damage, crowd control and utility. Use the full group when it saves time or unlocks worthwhile dungeon runs.

⏱️ Don't Wait

If one player has a short quest chain, profession trainer trip or gathering objective, the other can advance nearby. Regroup for dungeons, elites and major quest hubs.

Duo Combat Rules

1. Pull deliberately

Rogue starts the fight on the target that matters most. Shaman should not have to rescue every pull.

2. Interrupt priority

Rogue handles dangerous casts whenever practical. Shaman saves healing resources instead of allowing avoidable damage.

3. Keep momentum

When the Shaman is healthy on mana and the Rogue is stable, keep moving. Do not stop after every mob to fully reset.

4. Know when to split

If one player needs a profession trainer, class quest or isolated objective, split for a few minutes instead of dragging both characters through unnecessary travel.

Shared checklist: Before a dungeon, both players should have relevant quests, enough food/consumables, current profession skills and a quick repair/vendor plan. The Shaman should enter with a healing set; the Rogue should enter with current weapons and poisons.
