⛏️ Professions & Rogue Lockpicking

This tab is route-driven: keep gathering on the path, and use Lockpicking as a Rogue side-skill rather than a separate leveling grind. Lockpicking is capped at roughly 5 × character level in the Classic ruleset, so the practical goal is to keep it moving instead of trying to max it early. citeturn0search6

🗝️ Lockpicking rule: pick pockets whenever you are fighting humanoids, then use the appropriate footlockers/doors when your skill catches up. This keeps the skill useful without creating a huge XP detour.

🗡️ Rogue — Mining + Skinning

Gather naturally while moving. Keep Mining. Later transition Skinning → Engineering when the exact Forever recipe/trainer path is confirmed.

⚔️ World PvP Command Center

This is the PvP route layer for the Undead Rogue + Troll Shaman duo. It should tell us when PvP naturally intersects the leveling route, not send us on random detours looking for fights.

10–20
Early contested play

Watch Hillsbrad and nearby Horde/Alliance travel routes. Rogue stealth gives scouting and escape options; Shaman sustain helps the duo reset.

20–40
Core world-PvP window

Hillsbrad, Arathi and Stranglethorn routes naturally create more player contact. Protect quest momentum and choose fights deliberately.

40–60
Route-intersection PvP

Contested-zone travel, dungeon entrances and quest hubs become the priority. Don't abandon a strong XP route just to hunt players.
